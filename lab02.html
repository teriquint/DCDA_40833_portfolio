Lab 2: AI Tool Evaluation

Canva Background Remover

DCDA 40833 Skills Portfolio | Spring 2026

Lab Overview

For Lab 2, I tested Canva’s Background Remover tool. I used my own headshot and tried a few different background options to see how easy the tool was to use and how much it changed the look of the image.

I picked this tool because I already use Canva for design projects, so it felt like something real that I would actually use. This lab made me think more about how AI tools can save time, but still need a person to decide what looks best.

AI Tool Tested

Tool name: Canva Background Remover

Type of tool: AI photo editing and design tool

Purpose of the tool: This tool removes or changes the background of an image so the photo can be reused in a cleaner or more creative way.

Why I chose it: I chose Canva because it is easy to use and something I already work with. I wanted to see how well it could turn one regular photo into different versions for different uses.

My Process

I started with a headshot that had a city-style background. I uploaded it into Canva and used the background remover tool. After that, I tested a few new backgrounds to compare the results.

I made one version with a simple white background, one with a purple background, and one with a darker black and white background. Then I compared all of them side by side and looked closely at the edges, especially around the hair and shoulders.

Sample Test

For my sample, I used the same headshot and tested how Canva could change the background in different ways. This helped me see how much the same photo could change depending on the final style I wanted.

The white version looked the most professional to me. The purple version felt more fun and fit my portfolio better. The black and white version had a more dramatic feel. Seeing all of them together made it clear that the tool can help, but I still have to decide what version works best.

My Evaluation

What Worked Well

The best part was how easy it was. I did not need any advanced editing skills, and Canva did most of the work in just a few clicks. It made the photo feel more polished without taking a lot of time.

I also liked that I could get different looks from the same image. One looked more professional, one looked more creative, and one looked more dramatic. That made the tool feel useful for different kinds of projects.

What Did Not Work as Well

The tool was not perfect. I still had to look closely at the edges of the image, especially around the hair. That is where edited photos can start to look fake if the tool does not cut cleanly.

I also realized that the background changes the whole mood of the photo. Canva helped with the editing, but I still had to decide which version made the most sense for the purpose of the image.

What I Would Do Next Time

Next time, I would try a few more photos with different lighting and different backgrounds. I think that would help me see when the tool works really well and when it needs more cleanup.

Connection to Digital Culture

This connects to digital culture because AI is now part of everyday creative work. Something that used to take more time and skill can now be done very quickly. That makes design tools more accessible to more people.

At the same time, it also shows how easy it is to change images. A photo can be made to look more professional, more creative, or more dramatic in just a few minutes. That is helpful, but it also reminds me that edited images are everywhere and should be looked at carefully.

Lab 2 Reflection

What I learned: I learned that AI editing tools can save a lot of time and make design work easier. Canva made it simple to change the background and test different looks from the same photo.

What challenged me: The hardest part was deciding which version actually looked best. Even though the tool did the editing, I still had to use my own judgment to decide what looked natural and what fit the purpose.

How this connects to the course: This lab helped me see that AI is not only about writing or chat tools. It is also part of visual design and the way people create and share digital content every day.

My takeaway: AI can make the process faster, but it does not replace the person using it. I still had to make the final choices.
