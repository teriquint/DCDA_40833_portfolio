Portfolio Reflection

This portfolio shows how much I grew throughout the semester. I started the class without HTML experience, so even the early labs took me longer than I expected. Learning how pages, links, images, CSS, and GitHub worked together was challenging, but I kept working through it and became much more comfortable building and editing my own site.

As the semester continued, I started connecting more tools together. I used HTML and CSS to build the portfolio, Tableau to visualize data, Mapbox and Folium for mapping, and Python and Google Colab to clean, organize, and analyze data. The more I worked on the labs and my final project, the more I realized that digital work is not just about making something look nice. There has to be real thinking behind it.

One of my biggest takeaways is that visuals should help tell the story. Whether I was building charts, maps, critiques, or portfolio pages, I had to think about the viewer and what they needed to understand. I am still learning, but I feel proud of how far I came and how much more confident I am with digital tools than I was at the beginning of the semester.
