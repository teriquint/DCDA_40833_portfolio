Semester Reflection

What I learned: I am starting to learn how HTML works and how to edit code in Visual Studio Code. I also learned that each section of a webpage has a purpose and that small design choices can make a page feel more personal.

Challenges I overcame: One of the biggest challenges for me was becoming comfortable with the idea that I am not going to permanently mess something up. I worked through this by making a copy of my page so I could always go back if needed.

How I grew: I have grown by feeling more confident with HTML and understanding how placeholders, links, images, colors, and sections work together.

Looking ahead: These skills will definitely come in handy for my final project. I hope to get faster and more comfortable with coding as the semester continues.
